At a glance

Three rules to remember

3

Pages — exactly

The final submitted manuscript must occupy exactly three pages, using the official VLSI 2027 layout.

2 × 3

Fixed figure panes

Pages 2 and 3 use a mandatory grid of six panes per page; do not move or remove the pane boundaries.

8 pt

Minimum figure-label size

Text embedded in figures, graphs and illustrations must remain at least 8 points and readable.

Paper structure

How to arrange the three pages

Page 1

Main text & references

Primarily text in the prescribed two-column format. Include the title, authors, affiliations, abstract (60–150 words), main text, acknowledgements if applicable, and references. If needed, one pane on page 3 may hold additional references.

Pages 2 & 3
Figure paneFigure paneFigure paneFigure paneFigure paneFigure pane

Figures in fixed panes

Place illustrations inside the mandatory 2-column × 3-row grid. Do not modify the grid, erase pane borders or squeeze content into unreadably small labels.

Formatting & PDF quality

Before exporting your PDF

Follow all of the details in the official template, including margins, column dimensions, font sizes and figure placement. The checklist below highlights the most important requirements.

  • Use the paper size and margins specified by the selected VLSI template.
  • Use standard, embedded fonts. Times-family body text is recommended; Arial or Calibri is suitable for figures.
  • Keep the template's typographic hierarchy: 14 pt title, 12 pt author names and 10 pt main text.
  • Keep all text inside figures at or above 8 pt, with legible axis labels and captions.
  • Put figure captions below figures and table captions above tables.
  • Include DOI information for published references where available; remove active DOI hyperlinks as instructed for PDF eXpress.
  • Preserve the two-column first-page layout and the fixed figure-pane structure of pages 2–3.
  • Check the finished PDF for layout integrity and IEEE Xplore / PDF eXpress compatibility.
Use the complete templates as the authority: The VLSI PDF templates contain additional requirements and examples. These summary notes are not a substitute for them.
